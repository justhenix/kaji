## About

Kaji is a Windows companion for on-screen guidance and hands-free navigation. Use text or voice to ask for help, or control the cursor with your head.

- **Guide:** Highlights the next control and explains what to do.
- **Assist:** Prepares desktop actions for your approval.
- **Head control:** Moves the cursor with your head and lets you dwell to click.
